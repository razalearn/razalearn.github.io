Rollbook

Teacher planner for Android

Rollbook is the teacher planner that puts your whole school day on your phone: your class timetable, attendance register, lesson plans and syllabus, exams and marks, and student progress reports. It works fully offline, so it is ready in any classroom, with or without a signal.

What it does

  • Timetable. Your week on the home screen, today's periods first, with a reminder before each one.
  • Attendance register. Everyone starts present; you mark the exceptions.
  • Lesson plans and syllabus. Chapter by chapter, with periods taken against periods planned.
  • Exams and marks. A mark sheet for every paper, with totals against the pass mark.
  • Progress reports. A one-page PDF for any student, ready to share with parents.
  • Academic calendar. Terms, holidays and working days, counted for you.

Your classes, students, registers and marks are stored on your phone, not on our servers.
