Rollbook privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Developer: Short Budgets · App: Rollbook: Teacher Planner (com.shortbudgets.rollbook)

The short version

Rollbook is a planner for teachers. If you create your own account, we store who you are and which school you named, so that you can sign in again. Your classroom itself, meaning your classes, students, registers, marks, lesson plans and timetable, stays on your phone and is never sent to us. The app has no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What we collect when you create an account

When you tap Create account, the app sends the following to the Rollbook accounts service, and we keep it for as long as your account exists:

  • your name and mobile number, which is what you sign in with;
  • your password, which is stored only as a one-way hash that cannot be read back;
  • the name of your school, and its town or city, state or region and country;
  • your main subject and your email address, if you choose to enter them;
  • the version of the app and the make and model of the phone you signed in on, and when you last signed in.

We use this to sign you in, to let you sign in on a new phone, to answer you when you ask for help, and to understand which places and schools the app is being used in. We do not sell it, and we do not use it for advertising.

Location

The sign-up form has a button, Use my location to fill this in. Only if you tap it and allow the permission, the app reads your phone's approximate location once, uses Android's own address lookup to fill in your town, region and country, and sends the approximate coordinates with your sign-up so we know roughly where your school is. The app never reads your location at any other time and never in the background. You can leave the button alone and type your town instead.

What stays on your phone

For an account you created yourself, everything about your classroom is kept in a database on your phone: classes, subjects, students and their details, parent phone numbers, attendance registers, exam marks, lesson plans, your timetable, your calendar, your question bank and the progress reports you generate. None of it is uploaded to us and we cannot see it.

Backups are yours too. A backup is a file that the app saves where you tell it to: a folder on your phone, or wherever the system file picker reaches, such as your own Google Drive. If Android's own backup is switched on for your phone, Android may also include the app's database in the encrypted device backup held in your Google account; that is governed by Google's privacy policy and can be switched off in your phone's settings.

When you share a progress report, the app hands the PDF to the app you choose, for example WhatsApp or email. What happens to it after that is up to that app and the person you send it to.

If your school gave you a login

Some schools run Rollbook for their staff. If you sign in with a username your school issued, the app reads your classes, students and timetable from your school's own database and saves your registers, marks and other work back to it. Your school decides what is kept there and for how long, and is responsible for that data; this policy does not replace your school's. A copy is kept on your phone so that the app works without a connection. Ask your school if you want that data changed or deleted.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app reminds you before each period. These reminders are created on your phone from your own timetable. School accounts also show notices from the school.

Who else handles the data

The accounts service runs on Supabase, a database hosting provider, which stores the account details listed above on our behalf. Requests travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Like any internet service, the servers see the IP address a request comes from; we use it only to limit repeated sign-in attempts and do not keep it with your account. We share your details with nobody else, unless the law requires us to.

Students and children

Rollbook is made for teachers and is not directed at children. Students do not use the app. Details about students are entered by their teacher and, for an account you created yourself, stay on the teacher's phone as described above.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open More, tap your name at the top, then Delete account, and confirm with your password. That immediately and permanently removes your account, your sign-in sessions and the details listed above from our servers, and erases the app's data from your phone. If you can no longer use the app, see how to ask us to delete your account.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, removes everything stored on the phone but does not close your account.

Your choices

You can change your name, school and other details from your profile in the app. You can ask us for a copy of the account details we hold, or to correct or delete them, by writing to the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will appear here and the date at the top will be updated.
