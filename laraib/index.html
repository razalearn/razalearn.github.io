Laraib

Quran Hifz tracker and madrasa manager for Android

Laraib keeps every hifz student's Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil in one place, so teachers, parents and the madrasa always know where each student stands in their Quran memorization. It works offline and syncs when a connection is back.

What it does

  • Hifz tracking. Daily Sabaq, Sabqi and Manzil planned across the 604-page Madinah mushaf, with mistakes, weak pages, streaks, Daura and an estimate of when hifz will be complete.
  • Teachers. A whole hifz class on one screen: who recited today, who is behind, and 30-day reports for each student.
  • Madaris and institutes. Teachers join with an institution code; the institution sees every teacher's students and who needs attention.
  • Parents. Follow a child's progress at home, once the child or teacher approves.
  • Quran and Tajweed. The full Quran in the KFGQPC Hafs script, colour Tajweed, recitation, and 20 Tajweed lessons in English, Urdu and Hindi.
  • Learning to read. Arabic letters, harakat and reading courses, writing practice, and a tasbeeh counter.

Support

Found a problem, or have an idea for the app? Email razakatihari@gmail.com and include your phone model and what you were doing. We read every message.

If your madrasa or teacher manages your account, ask them first about joining a class or institution.
