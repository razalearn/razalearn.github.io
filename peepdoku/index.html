Peepdoku

Chick logic puzzle for Android

Place one cute chick in every colour. Every chick has exactly one right place on the board, and pure deduction will lead you there: no guessing, no luck, just calm and satisfying logic puzzles. Every level is built on your phone, so the whole game plays offline.

How to play

Each board is split into coloured regions. Place exactly one chick in every row, every column and every colour, and no two chicks may touch, not even diagonally. Stuck? A hint places a chick for you, or explains the next logical step.
