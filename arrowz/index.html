Arrowz

Arrow maze puzzle for Android

Arrowz is a calm arrow puzzle about finding the way out. Every board is a maze of arrows, each bent into its own path and pointing its own way. Tap the arrows away in the right order and watch a crowded grid turn into a clean, empty board. There is no timer and no rush, and the whole game plays offline.

How to play

Tap an arrow and it slides out the way its head points, following its own path. It can only leave when nothing stands between its head and the edge, so send away the arrows in front first. Tapping a blocked arrow costs a heart. Stuck? The bulb lights up an arrow that can safely leave, and the # button draws guide lines across a busy board.
